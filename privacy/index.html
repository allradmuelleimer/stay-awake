Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

In short

No account, no sign-in, no server of our own. The app stores sessions, schedules and settings on your device only. Wake checks work entirely without internet.

Three Google services are built in: advertising (Google AdMob, free version only), purchases (Google Play) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). What each of them receives is set out below. Beyond that there is no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

So that Stay Awake remembers your choices and can resume a running session even after the phone restarts, the app keeps the following in its private storage:

  • your settings: chosen confirmation actions, check interval, last used session mode, duration or end time, appearance, app language, and whether onboarding is complete
  • your schedules with times and weekdays
  • the state of the currently running session (start, planned end, time of the next check, last confirmation). It is deleted when the session ends — the app keeps no history of past sessions.
  • a cached copy of your purchase status (whether and which Pro option is active, and when Google Play last confirmed it), so ads stay off even without a connection

None of this is sent to us. We run no server, and there is no user account.

Motion sensor

If you choose Shake or Flip as your confirmation, the app reads the phone’s accelerometer during a running session to recognise that gesture — also with the screen off. The readings are evaluated in memory only, never stored and never transmitted. When the session ends, the app stops listening. If neither Shake nor Flip is selected, the sensor is not read.

Volume buttons

If you chose the volume button as your confirmation, the app registers the key press while it is in the foreground and a session is running. It only notes that a button was pressed; nothing about it is stored.

What the app does not use

Stay Awake does not access the microphone, camera, location, contacts, health data or your files, and requests no permission for them. A phone-state permission that Google’s ad library brings along by default has been explicitly removed from the app.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows a banner on the home screen, during a running session and in Settings. Ads never appear on the check or alarm screen. The banner is delivered by Google AdMob (the “Next-Gen” Google Mobile Ads SDK), a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Depending on your consent, Google processes your device’s advertising ID, technical device information (such as model, operating system and language), the IP address and an approximate location derived from it, and usage data about the ads (for example impressions and clicks). The purpose is to select and deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and — if you agree — personalise ads. Google processes some of this data as an independent controller and may share it with advertising partners.

The ads never see your sessions. Schedules, intervals, confirmations and sensor data are never handed to the ad library.

Consent: Where the law requires it — in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — a dialog from Google (User Messaging Platform) asks before the first ad whether and how your data may be used for advertising. The dialog never appears during a check or alarm. Which ads you see afterwards depends on your choice; if your answer means no ads may be requested, the banner stays empty.

Withdrawal: You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Settings → Info → Privacy → “Change ad consent”. Withdrawal takes effect for the future. You can also reset or delete your device’s advertising ID in the Android settings.

Stay Awake Pro removes ads everywhere, instantly. With Pro, the app loads no ads and shows no consent dialog.

More from Google: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Google ad settings

Purchases through Google Play

Stay Awake Pro is available as a monthly subscription or as a one-time purchase. Both run exclusively through Google Play (Google Play Billing). You enter payment details with Google only; Stay Awake never sees them. From Google Play the app only learns whether a valid purchase exists and of which kind, and acknowledges the purchase to Google Play as required. You manage and cancel subscriptions in Google Play. Payment processing is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends a technical report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service. This happens only in the versions published on Google Play; test builds send nothing.

A report contains the stack trace (where in the program the error occurred), device model, Android version, app version, the time, and a randomly generated Crashlytics installation ID that lets several crashes on the same device be grouped. The IP address is transmitted as a technical necessity of the connection. The report contains no session or schedule data, and no advertising ID or purchase data.

Its sole purpose is to find and fix bugs so that checks arrive reliably. Google processes this data as our processor. More: Privacy and security in Firebase.

No analytics, no tracking

Stay Awake includes no analytics services, does not measure usage and builds no profiles. Apart from the three Google services named above, nobody receives data from the app.

Legal bases

  • App features (settings, schedules, session state, sensor evaluation): Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — they are needed to provide the function you asked for. This data never leaves your device.
  • Advertising: your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where your device is accessed, § 25(1) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). Where no consent is required, delivering non-personalised ads rests on our legitimate interest in financing the free version (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Purchases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).
  • Crash reports: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in fixing bugs and providing an app whose wake function works reliably.
  • This website: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure delivery).

Transfers to third countries

Google may process data from advertising, purchases and crash reports outside the EU as well, in particular in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; in addition, Google uses the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention

  • Data on the device remains until you change it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in the Android settings. The state of a session is deleted as soon as the session ends.
  • Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days, according to Google.
  • Advertising and purchase data is kept by Google under its own retention periods; see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.
  • We ourselves store nothing. If you email us, we keep the message for as long as needed to handle it.

Backup and changing devices

The app takes part in the regular Android backup. If you have backup turned on in your Google account, Android may store the app’s settings, schedules and cached purchase status there and restore them on a new device. Google runs that backup on your behalf; we have no access to it. Whether it runs is up to you in the Android settings.

Deletion

All app data lives in the app’s private storage. It is deleted completely when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in the Android settings. You can delete individual schedules directly in the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

Because we hold no data about you, you exercise most of these rights directly on your device: change or delete settings and schedules in the app, and everything goes when you uninstall it. You change your advertising consent under Settings → Info → Privacy → “Change ad consent”. For data Google processes as an independent controller, you can also contact Google directly. For anything else, write to us at the address above.

Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example in your country of residence, or with the authority responsible for us, the Data Protection Commissioner of Lower Saxony (Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen).

Children

Stay Awake is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Teenagers under 16 should only agree to personalised advertising with their parents’ permission.

Changes

If the app changes so that different data is processed, we will update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or in the Play Store listing. The date of the current version is always shown at the top.

This website

This site sets no cookies, loads no fonts or scripts from third-party servers and measures nothing — which is also why there is no cookie banner. When a page is requested, the host GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) processes technically necessary connection data (IP address, timestamp, file requested) in order to deliver the page and defend against attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
