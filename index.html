Why a normal alarm isn’t enough

An alarm is built to wake you up once. Staying awake means getting through hour after hour – and the hard part is the moment nobody checks on you.

A normal alarm

  • Rings once – at a time you set in advance.
  • Switched off half asleep, and nothing comes after it.
  • Never asks whether you’re still awake.

Stay Awake

  • Asks again and again, all night – at your interval, from one minute to twelve hours.
  • Asks quietly first. Only if you don’t answer does it vibrate, then ring.
  • The alarm rings until you answer. Then the next round starts.

What happens when a check is due

Soft first, loud only if needed. Every step stops the moment you answer.

  1. 0 sThe questionThe screen lights up – over the lock screen too – and asks: Are you still awake?
  2. 10 sVibrationNo answer yet? The phone starts to vibrate.
  3. 20 sAlarmStill nothing: a real alarm with your phone’s alarm sound.
  4. YesNext roundOne tap or one shake: everything stops and the full interval starts again.

Start, confirm, schedule, check

  • Start

    Two decisions, one button

    Unlimited or Timer, and how often you want to be asked. Turn the dial – from one minute up to twelve hours, 30 minutes to start with. The app remembers your last choice.

    The timer runs for a set time (“for 2 hours”) or until a clock time (“until 5:30”).

  • Confirm

    Tap, shake, flip – your choice

    The big Yes always works. In Settings you can add shaking the phone, flipping it face down and back, or a volume button – in any combination. Shake and flip also work with the screen off.

    You don’t have to wait for the question: confirm whenever you like, and the interval starts over from that moment. The session notification has an I am awake button too.

  • Shifts

    Schedules that start on their own

    For nights that repeat: start and end time, optional weekdays, interval. The session starts by itself at the set time and ends at the end time.

    Without a weekday it runs once, at the next start.

Questions and answers

How is this different from a normal alarm clock?

An alarm clock rings at a time, whether you are awake or not. Stay Awake asks first and only gets loud if you do not answer. It is built for staying awake, not for waking up in the morning.

Do I have to keep the screen on?

No. Put the phone down, lock it, let the screen go dark. The check appears over the lock screen. If you chose shaking, a shake confirms even with the screen off.

Which alarm sound does it use?

By default your phone’s own alarm sound – the one you are used to waking up to. In Settings you can switch to the Stay Awake melody or pick another sound.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Checks, vibration and the alarm run entirely on the phone.

What does it cost?

Everything that keeps you awake is free. The free version shows a small banner on normal screens, never on the check or the alarm. Stay Awake Pro removes it, monthly or once; the price is shown in Google Play.

Is there an iPhone version?

No, Stay Awake is only available for Android.
